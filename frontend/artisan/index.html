Help & support

Find answers to common questions about using KalaSetu.

How do I add a product?

Open Products from your dashboard and use the available product creation option. Enter your product details and review the information before saving.

How can I use the AI tools?

Open the relevant AI tool, provide the requested product information, and review its suggestions before using them. AI-generated results should be checked for accuracy.

How do I change my language?

Select your preferred language in Language preferences and save your choice. Supported interface text will change as the translation system is connected to each page.

What if I find a problem?

If a feature does not work as expected, note what you were doing and the message displayed. Use the support channel provided by the KalaSetu team when available.
